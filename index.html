How it’s made

Everything
touches fire.

Four fires, one kitchen. Each does one thing — and does it every day, without fail.

01

Tandoor

Clay oven at 480°C. Breads, tikkas, and the smoke that gets under everything we serve.

02

Sigri

Charcoal grill lit at three in the afternoon. Seekh, chops, the char you can smell from the street.

03

Handi

Sealed clay pot, slow heat, four hours. Korma, nihari, gravies that taste of patience.

04

The Pass

Marble counter, one chef, forty covers. Every plate leaves this surface in under a minute.
