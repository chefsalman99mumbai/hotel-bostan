The Idea

Hotel Bostan — a Halal kitchen in Jogeshwari West. Where the fire is old and the welcome older still. Since 1980, everything on the menu has begun within a few hours of this table, and nothing leaves the kitchen until it has met one of four fires.

For over four decades the dining hall has been built around a single long table and a working marble pass, so guests watch the last thirty seconds of every plate come together. It is a kitchen that behaves, quietly, like the fire it cooks over: charcoal, salt, patience — and a welcome that never closes.

From the Fire

Four fires, one kitchen. Each does one thing — and does it every day, without fail.

01

Tandoor

Clay oven at 480°C. Breads, tikkas, and the smoke that gets under everything we serve.

02

Sigri

Charcoal grill lit at three in the afternoon. Seekh, chops, the char you can smell from the street.

03

Handi

Sealed clay pot, slow heat, four hours. Korma, nihari, gravies that taste of patience.

04

The Pass

Marble counter, one chef, forty covers. Every plate leaves this surface in under a minute.

A note on sourcing & kitchen

Produce arrives daily from Nashik and Pune. Ghee is hand-churned in Rajasthan. Citrus comes from Nagpur, sea salt from Kutch, and the herbs — curry leaf, mint, coriander, bay — are grown on our own roof, four floors above the dining hall. Our kitchen is purely Halal; we do not serve alcohol or pork, and we open every day of the week.

The Dining Hall

Forty covers across one long table and a run of two-tops, all facing a working marble pass. Candlelight, exposed brick, brass, and a barrel-vaulted ceiling that keeps the noise low and the hall warm.

The Alcove — twelve seats behind a closing door — is available for private dinners, tastings and press previews. Menus are written to the season and confirmed a week ahead.
